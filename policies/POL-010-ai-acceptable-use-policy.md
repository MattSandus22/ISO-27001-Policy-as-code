# AI Acceptable Use Policy

> **Draft.** Under review by the ISMS Steering Committee. Until approved, POL-003 and POL-004 apply to AI tools.

## Purpose

To let staff benefit from generative AI tools while protecting Northwind and customer information, and to make sure AI-assisted work stays accurate and accountable. This policy supports the AI risk register and aligns with ISO/IEC 42001.

## Scope

All use of AI tools, including chat assistants, coding assistants and AI features inside SaaS products, by Northwind staff and contractors for Northwind work.

## Policy statements

- **AIU-01** Only AI tools on the approved list shall be used with Northwind information; tools are approved through the supplier process in POL-006.
- **AIU-02** Confidential information, including customer personal data and secrets, must not be entered into any AI tool unless that tool is approved for Confidential data.
- **AIU-03** Staff shall review AI-generated output for accuracy before relying on it, and remain accountable for work they submit.
- **AIU-04** AI-generated code shall go through the same review and security testing as any other code (POL-007).
- **AIU-05** Customer-facing AI features must tell customers they are interacting with AI and offer a route to a human.
- **AIU-06** New AI use cases that make or support decisions about people shall be registered in the AI risk register and impact-assessed before use.

## Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| AI Governance Lead | Owns this policy and the approved AI tools list |
| Security team | Assesses AI tools and providers |
| All staff | Use AI tools in line with this policy |

## Exceptions and enforcement

Exceptions require approval from the AI Governance Lead and the CISO. Entering Confidential information into an unapproved tool is a security incident and must be reported under POL-005.
