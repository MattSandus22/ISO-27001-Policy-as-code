# Information Security Policy

## Purpose

This policy sets Northwind's commitment to protecting the confidentiality, integrity and availability of information, and establishes the information security management system (ISMS) that all other security policies sit beneath.

## Scope

All Northwind employees, contractors and third parties who access Northwind information or systems, and all information assets within the ISMS scope: the e-commerce platform, corporate IT and supporting cloud services.

## Policy statements

- **ISP-01** Northwind shall maintain an ISMS that conforms to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and is reviewed by top management at least annually.
- **ISP-02** Information security objectives shall be set annually, be measurable, and be reported to the ISMS Steering Committee each quarter.
- **ISP-03** Every information asset and security process shall have a named owner who is accountable for its protection.
- **ISP-04** Duties that could allow one person to commit and conceal fraud or error shall be segregated, or compensating monitoring shall be in place.
- **ISP-05** Information security shall be assessed at the start of every project that introduces or changes systems processing Internal or Confidential information.
- **ISP-06** The CISO shall maintain current contacts with relevant authorities, regulators and industry security groups.
- **ISP-07** Legal, regulatory and contractual security requirements shall be identified, recorded in the compliance register and reviewed annually.
- **ISP-08** The ISMS shall be independently reviewed at planned intervals and after significant changes.
- **ISP-09** Compliance with security policies shall be checked regularly by managers and through the internal audit programme.
- **ISP-10** All topic-specific security policies must be approved by management, published to staff, and reviewed at least annually.

## Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Chief Executive Officer | Approves this policy and provides resources for the ISMS |
| ISMS Steering Committee | Sets objectives, reviews performance, accepts residual risk |
| CISO | Owns the ISMS and this policy; reports to the Steering Committee |
| Managers | Ensure their teams follow security policies |
| All staff | Read, acknowledge and follow security policies; report concerns |

## Exceptions and enforcement

Exceptions must be requested through the security exception process, risk-assessed, approved by the CISO, and time-limited to a maximum of 12 months. Breaches of this policy may result in disciplinary action under the HR disciplinary process (see POL-011).

## Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually and after any significant change to the business, threat landscape or legal requirements.
