# Physical Security Policy

## Purpose

To protect Northwind offices, equipment and the information in them from unauthorized physical access, damage and theft.

## Scope

Northwind's head office and any other location holding in-scope equipment or information. Production infrastructure is hosted by AWS and is covered by supplier assurance (POL-006).

## Policy statements

- **PHY-01** Office perimeters shall be defined, with entry only through controlled doors.
- **PHY-02** Entry shall require an individual access badge; visitors must sign in, wear a visitor badge and be escorted.
- **PHY-03** Rooms holding network equipment or Confidential records shall be locked, with access limited to named staff.
- **PHY-04** Entrances and equipment rooms shall be covered by CCTV, with footage kept for 30 days.
- **PHY-05** Equipment rooms shall have fire detection and protection appropriate to the equipment.
- **PHY-06** Work in secure areas shall be limited to authorised staff, and lone working there must be avoided where possible.
- **PHY-07** Equipment shall be placed to reduce the risk of damage and of screens being overlooked in public areas.
- **PHY-08** Equipment containing storage media shall be wiped or destroyed using an approved method before disposal or reuse, with a certificate of destruction kept.

## Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Head of Facilities | Owns this policy, access badges and CCTV |
| Reception | Visitor sign-in and badges |
| Head of IT | Secure disposal of IT equipment |

## Exceptions and enforcement

Exceptions require approval from the Head of Facilities and the CISO. Badge sharing or tailgating is a policy breach.
