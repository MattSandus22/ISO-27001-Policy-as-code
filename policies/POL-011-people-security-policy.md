# People Security Policy

## Purpose

To make sure employees and contractors are suitable for their roles, understand their security responsibilities, and keep those responsibilities after they leave.

## Scope

All employees and contractors, from recruitment through to leaving Northwind.

## Policy statements

- **PSP-01** Background checks proportionate to the role and permitted by local law shall be completed before access to Confidential information is granted.
- **PSP-02** Employment contracts and contractor agreements shall state information security and confidentiality responsibilities.
- **PSP-03** Staff who handle Confidential information, and all contractors with system access, must sign a confidentiality agreement before starting.
- **PSP-04** All staff shall complete security awareness training on joining and annually after that, with role-based training for engineers and administrators.
- **PSP-05** Phishing simulations shall run at least quarterly, and results shall be reported to the ISMS Steering Committee.
- **PSP-06** A formal disciplinary process shall apply to security policy breaches, consistent with the HR disciplinary procedure.
- **PSP-07** Confidentiality obligations that continue after employment ends shall be communicated to leavers during offboarding.

## Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Chief People Officer | Owns this policy, screening and HR processes |
| Security team | Designs training and phishing simulations |
| Line managers | Make sure their staff complete training |

## Exceptions and enforcement

Screening exceptions require CISO approval and restricted access until checks complete. Training completion is tracked; access may be suspended for staff more than 30 days overdue.
