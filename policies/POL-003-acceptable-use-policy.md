# Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose

To set clear rules for using Northwind devices, accounts and information, so that everyday work does not create avoidable security risk.

## Scope

Everyone who uses Northwind devices, accounts or information, whether in the office, at home or travelling.

## Policy statements

- **AUP-01** Northwind devices and accounts shall be used primarily for business purposes; limited personal use is allowed if it does not breach this policy.
- **AUP-02** Company laptops must have full-disk encryption, endpoint protection and automatic updates enabled, and must lock after five minutes of inactivity.
- **AUP-03** Only software from the approved catalogue shall be installed on company devices.
- **AUP-04** Users must lock their screen when leaving a device unattended and keep Confidential papers out of sight when not in use.
- **AUP-05** When working remotely, users shall use trusted networks or the company VPN and must not let others use their device.
- **AUP-06** Devices taken off-premises shall be kept under the user's control and never left in vehicles.
- **AUP-07** Users shall return all company devices, tokens and information on or before their last day.
- **AUP-08** Software, content and data shall be used only in line with their licences and copyright.
- **AUP-09** Access to known malicious and high-risk website categories shall be blocked by web filtering on company devices.
- **AUP-10** Users must report lost or stolen devices to the IT service desk immediately.

## Roles and responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| All users | Follow this policy and acknowledge it annually |
| Head of IT | Owns device standards, software catalogue and web filtering |
| Line managers | Make sure leavers return assets |

## Exceptions and enforcement

Exceptions require approval from the Head of IT and the CISO. Misuse may lead to removal of access and disciplinary action.
